        Spring conditions

        Where to find the blossoms

        Chidorigafuchi in Tokyo, the Kamo River in Kyoto, and Osaka Castle Park. Late March to early April is the usual city window. Check the forecast near departure and let the flowers decide which park you visit.

        Tokyo blossom guide
        Driving in the mountains

        Ask for winter tires in March and check the rental branch’s advice for April. Look up live road conditions before rural drives and arrive at your inn in daylight. With a U.S. license, bring a paper 1949 Geneva Convention permit, your license, and your passport.

        NEXCO road conditionsJapan’s driving permit rules
        If it rains

        Swap in the Kiso lacquerware center or the Kashimo Meijiza theater. If the roads are bad, Nagoya has trains to Kiso and Hida, and highway buses to Gujo.
